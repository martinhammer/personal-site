In a half-unofficial way, Microsoft offers a driver and a small utility to mount ISO CD-ROM images as a drive in Windows XP and Vista. It can be downloaded from a [posting on Microsoft support site](http://support.microsoft.com/kb/916902) (here is the link [directly to the self-extracting archive](http://download.microsoft.com/download/7/b/6/7b6abd84-7841-4978-96f5-bd58df02efa2/winxpvirtualcdcontrolpanel_21.exe)). The instructions are included in the "readme.txt" file. Although the program comes without any support from Microsoft it seemed to work fine for me.
