For some reason Unity in Ubuntu 16.04 and the SublimeText deb package don't play well together and the integration is not quite right - for one the launcher says "UNREGISTERED" even after entering a valid licence key, and SublimeText does not appear in Nautilus context menu as an option to open files. [Here's the workaround](http://askubuntu.com/a/755041) for this issue.
