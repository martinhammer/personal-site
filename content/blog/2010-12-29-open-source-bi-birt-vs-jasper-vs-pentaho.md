In case you missed these, [Innovent Solutions](http://www.innoventsolutions.com) (one of the [corporate backers](http://www.eclipse.org/birt/phoenix/project/pmc.php#pmc) of Eclipse BIRT project) published papers comparing [Jasper](http://jasperforge.org/) and [Pentaho](http://www.pentaho.org/) to [BIRT](http://www.eclipse.org/birt/phoenix/). Naturally, they conclude that BIRT is top of the pile in open source reporting, however they do justify this conclusion and the papers make an interesting read.

- [BIRT versus Jasper](http://www.innoventsolutions.com/birt-vs-jasper.html)
- [BIRT versus Pentaho](http://www.innoventsolutions.com/birt-vs-pentaho.html)
- [OSS Reporting Comparison Matrix](http://www.innoventsolutions.com/birt-jasper-pentaho-comparison-matrix.html)
