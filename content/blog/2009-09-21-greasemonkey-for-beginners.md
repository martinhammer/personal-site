[Tuxradar](http://www.tuxradar.com), a site worth keeping an eye on, published a basic [Greasemonkey for beginners](http://www.tuxradar.com/content/greasemonkey-beginners) tutorial a little while back. Greasemonkey is a Firefox plugin which allows you to add little scripts which are executed when you browse to a particular page. The script can manipulate the page itself, e.g. to show additional content or to hide annoying ads or whitespace, change style and much much more. Many thousands of scripts have been published and if you know Javascript and browser scripting it's very easy to created your own. Here's a couple of other Greasemonkey links:

- [Greasemonkey for beginners](http://www.tuxradar.com/content/greasemonkey-beginners) (Tuxradar)

- [Greasespot](http://www.greasespot.net/)

- [Dive Into Greasemonkey](http://diveintogreasemonkey.org/) (the definite reference)

- [userscripts.org](http://userscripts.org/) (a script repository)
